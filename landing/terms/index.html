The one thing to take away

Brasstally prepares books and drafts returns. It is not an accountant, it does not give advice, and it cannot file anything with the CRA. A draft is a starting point for you or your accountant, and the numbers remain your responsibility.

Agreeing to these terms

Using Brasstally means agreeing to what is on this page. If you are using it for a company, you are confirming you are allowed to accept on that company's behalf.

What Brasstally is

Bookkeeping software. It records entries, reads receipts, tracks what you owe and are owed, connects to your bank to reconcile, and prepares summaries and drafts for tax purposes.

What it is not

  • Not an accountant. Nothing in the app is accounting, tax or legal advice. It shows its working so a professional can check it, which is not the same as replacing one.
  • Not a filing service. No Canadian tax software exposes a filing interface to third parties. Brasstally prepares the package and tracks the return through draft, sent, filed and assessed. You or your accountant file it.
  • Not a bank or a payment service. It never moves money. A bank connection is read only.
  • Not a guarantee of accuracy. Categories are suggested, tax treatment is derived from rules, and a receipt is read by a model. All of it is editable because all of it can be wrong. The figures you file are yours.
