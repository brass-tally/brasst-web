03
Bank‑true

When the books drift, tell them the truth and move on.

Every other ledger quietly assumes you never miss a week. This one assumes you do. Set the balance to what the bank actually says on the day it said it — the months you never tracked stop dragging on the number, and only what you log afterwards counts. Every anchor is kept in the history, so nothing is quietly rewritten. Connect the bank through Plaid and new transactions arrive as drafts you confirm, same as everything else.
