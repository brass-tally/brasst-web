Capture

Photograph a receipt. Confirm four fields.

The app reads the receipt and returns a draft with the amount, date, description, and category. Edit what's wrong, then save. Nothing posts without you.

02
Two books

The company's money and yours, kept apart.

Pay yourself in one motion and both sides get written together: salary is an expense here and income there. All books under one sign-in.

03
Bank-true

Anchor to the real balance and move on.

Set the balance to what the bank says on any day, and the months you missed stop dragging on the number. Every anchor stays in history. Connect Plaid and new transactions arrive as drafts you confirm.

Tax day

Year-end is a tax package your accountant files from.

Pick your year-end and build the package. The app maps your categories to CRA forms, computes all deadlines, and emails everything to your accountant.

Also included: recurring payables · 30 and 90-day cash calendar · receipt attachments · full anchor history · linked transfers · personal ledger with T1 dates.
